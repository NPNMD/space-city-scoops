# Space City Scoops Logo - Art Direction Prompt

## Quick Summary (TL;DR)

Create a **retro pixel art logo** for "Space City Scoops" - a premium freeze-dried ice cream brand. The logo should feature an **anthropomorphic ice cream cone mascot** (NOT astronaut/alien) in 8-bit/16-bit gaming aesthetic with ice cream flavor color palette (pinks, creams, browns, greens). Must be fun, nostalgic, and clearly communicate "premium ice cream brand" - NOT a space game.

**Deliverables**: PNG with transparency, 512x512px (primary) and 256x256px (favicon)

---

## Detailed Art Direction

### Brand Overview

**Brand Name**: Space City Scoops  
**Product**: Premium freeze-dried ice cream sold in limited-edition drops  
**Brand Positioning**: Retro-futuristic ice cream shop meets artisanal food culture  
**Target Audience**: 18-45 year olds who appreciate nostalgia, gaming aesthetics, and exclusive products

**Critical**: This is an **ICE CREAM E-COMMERCE BRAND**, not a video game. The retro pixel art style is purely aesthetic - the product is real, physical ice cream.

---

### Visual Concept

#### Primary Logo Element: Ice Cream Cone Mascot

**NOT**: Astronaut, alien, rocket ship, UFO  
**YES**: Friendly, anthropomorphic ice cream cone character

**Character Design**:
- **Body**: Classic waffle cone texture (pixel art cross-hatch pattern)
- **Ice Cream Scoops**: 2-3 colorful scoops stacked on top
  - Suggested flavors to represent: Strawberry (pink), Vanilla (cream), Chocolate (brown), or Mint (green)
  - Each scoop should have personality and appeal
- **Face**: Simple, friendly expression
  - Large dot eyes (2-4 pixels each)
  - Small smile or happy expression
  - Optional: Small pixel blush marks for extra charm
- **Arms/Hands** (optional): Small pixel art arms, possibly giving thumbs up or waving
- **Pose**: Standing upright, confident, welcoming

**Style Reference**: Think classic 8-bit Nintendo/SNES character design - bold outlines, limited color palette per element, chunky pixels, expressive despite simplicity.

---

### Text Treatment

#### Primary Text: "SPACE CITY SCOOPS"

**Typography**:
- Chunky pixel font (similar to "Press Start 2P" style)
- Bold, readable, highly legible
- Each letter should feel substantial and blocky
- Consistent pixel size (no sub-pixel rendering)

**Color**:
- Primary text color: White (#FFFFFF)
- Outline/stroke: Colorful border (2-4 pixels thick)
  - Suggested outline colors: Gradient or multi-color (pink → cream → brown to represent ice cream)
  - Alternative: Single bold color outline (strawberry pink #FF6B9D)

**Optional Effects**:
- Small pixelated drips/melting effect on select letters (creates ice cream association)
- Drips should be subtle, not overwhelming
- Color drips to match ice cream scoop colors

#### Subtitle: "Premium Freeze-Dried Ice Cream"

**Typography**:
- Smaller pixel font (approximately 1/3 size of main text)
- Can be same font family or complementary pixel font
- Less prominent but still readable

**Color**:
- Light gray (#E0E0E0) or white
- Should not compete with main brand name

**Placement**: Below main logo text, centered

---

### Color Palette

**Primary Ice Cream Colors** (use 2-4 of these):
- Strawberry Pink: #FF6B9D
- Vanilla Cream: #FFF5E1
- Chocolate Brown: #5C4033 or #3D2817
- Mint Green: #88B04B or #D5F4E6
- Neapolitan: Combination of pink, white/cream, and brown

**Accent/UI Colors**:
- Pure White: #FFFFFF (text, highlights)
- Deep Black: #0A0A0A (shadows, outlines)
- Light Gray: #E0E0E0 (secondary text)

**Color Strategy**:
- Use vibrant, appetizing ice cream colors
- Avoid muddy or dull tones
- Each color should feel "tasty" and premium
- Maintain sufficient contrast for readability

**What to AVOID**:
- Space colors (deep blues, neon purples, cosmic gradients)
- Sci-fi metallics (silver, chrome effects)
- Dark, moody palettes
- Colors that don't evoke food/ice cream

---

### Technical Specifications

#### File Formats & Sizes

**Primary Logo**:
- Dimensions: 512x512 pixels
- Format: PNG with transparency (32-bit)
- Color mode: RGB
- Background: Transparent
- DPI: 72 (web) or 300 (print-ready version)

**Favicon Version**:
- Dimensions: 256x256 pixels
- Same specifications as above
- Simplified if needed for clarity at small size
- Consider icon-only version (just the mascot, no text)

**File Naming**:
- `logo.png` (512x512)
- `logo-favicon.png` (256x256)
- `logo-icon-only.png` (optional, mascot without text)

#### Design Guidelines

**Pixel Perfection**:
- All elements must align to pixel grid (no anti-aliasing blur)
- Sharp, crisp edges
- Consistent pixel sizing throughout
- Each "pixel" in the design should be clearly visible (aim for 8x8 or 16x16 base unit)

**Border/Outline**:
- Logo should work on both light and dark backgrounds
- Consider 2-4px outer stroke/border for versatility
- Primary design should optimally display on dark backgrounds (#0A0A0A)

**Scalability**:
- Logo must remain legible at 32x32 pixels (favicon size)
- Key elements (mascot, brand name) should be recognizable even when small
- Avoid excessive detail that disappears at small sizes

---

### What to AVOID (Critical)

#### Space/Sci-Fi Elements That Confuse the Brand:
- ❌ Astronauts, aliens, or extraterrestrials
- ❌ Rocket ships, UFOs, flying saucers
- ❌ Stars, planets, cosmic backgrounds
- ❌ Space helmets or suits
- ❌ Nebula clouds or galaxy effects
- ❌ Laser beams or sci-fi weapons
- ❌ Futuristic tech elements

#### Gaming Elements to Avoid:
- ❌ Health bars, HP indicators
- ❌ Weapons or battle imagery
- ❌ Score displays or game UI elements
- ❌ "Level up" or "boss battle" imagery
- ❌ Pixel explosions or combat effects

#### Why: 
The brand name includes "Space City" (Houston reference) but the actual product is **ice cream**. Previous confusion has occurred when designs focus too heavily on space themes, making customers think it's a game instead of a food product.

**The mascot is an ICE CREAM CONE from Space City (Houston), NOT an astronaut.**
